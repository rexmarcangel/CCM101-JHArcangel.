## 1.	Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
The cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS (Amazon Web Services). I liked it because it had a lot of services available for computing, storage, databases, security, and more. I appreciated more how many tools can be used to help a business or organization.

## 2.	What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP? 
AWS, Azure, and GCP all provide cloud services such as computing, storage, databases, networking, and security. They all have scalable resources that can be increased or decreased depending on user needs. Although their interfaces, service names, and features differ, they share the same goal of providing reliable and flexible cloud solutions.

## 3.	How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection? 
Business requirements have a significant impact on the choice of cloud platform. Budget, security, performance, scalability, and specific business needs should be considered. For example, if a company needs large storage or high computing power, it should choose a platform that can provide this at a reasonable cost.
